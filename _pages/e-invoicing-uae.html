How it works

How UAE e-invoicing works, in plain English

The UAE uses a five-corner model on the Peppol network. You don't need to learn it, but it helps to know who does what.

  1. Corner 1 · You

    You issue the invoice

    In Finasheet, or in your connected ERP.

  2. Corner 2 · Your ASP

    Our ASP partner checks and sends it

    As a PINT-AE e-invoice over Peppol.

  3. Corner 3 · Their ASP

    Your customer's ASP receives it

    Every business in scope has its own ASP.

  4. Corner 4 · Customer

    It lands in their system

    Ready to process, with no re-typing.

  5. Corner 5 · FTA

    Tax data goes to the FTA

    Reported through the ASPs.

One ASP for both directionsThe FTA requires each business to use the same ASP for sending and receiving e-invoices.
No more cancelling invoicesThere is no invoice cancellation in UAE e-invoicing. Mistakes are fixed with a credit note.
No XML for your teamYour team works on normal invoice screens. The technical format is handled by the software and the ASP.

Regulation

UAE e-invoicing deadlines and key rules

The deadlines depend on your revenue and on whether you are a government entity. Appointing an ASP comes first; mandatory e-invoicing starts later.

Source: Ministerial Decision No. 244 of 2025 as amended by Ministerial Decision No. 66 of 2026. Revenue means gross income in your most recent accounting period. Last checked 6 November 2026.
WhoAppoint an ASP byMandatory e-invoicing from
Businesses with revenue below AED 50 million31 March 20271 July 2027
Businesses with revenue of AED 50 million or more30 October 2026 (passed)1 January 2027
Government entities (B2G)31 March 20271 October 2027

Who is in scope

Business-to-business (B2B) and business-to-government (B2G) transactions, whether or not you are VAT registered. Business-to-consumer (B2C) sales are excluded.

Not VAT registered?

You use a Tax Identification Number (TIN). For VAT-registered businesses, the TIN is the first 10 digits of your TRN.

PDFs don't count

PDF, Word, image, scanned or emailed invoices are not e-invoices. E-invoices are structured data in the PINT-AE format.

Penalties

Under Cabinet Decision No. 173 of 2026, not implementing e-invoicing or not appointing an ASP on time can cost AED 5,000, then AED 5,000 a month (capped at AED 50,000 in any 12 months). Late e-invoices and credit notes can cost AED 100 each, capped at AED 5,000 a month.

  • Federal Tax Authority: UAE e-invoicing

FAQ

UAE e-invoicing questions, answered

What is UAE e-invoicing?

UAE e-invoicing is the national system for issuing and receiving business invoices as structured data rather than PDFs or paper. Invoices are created in the PINT-AE format, exchanged between businesses through Accredited Service Providers (ASPs) over the Peppol network, and the tax data is reported to the Federal Tax Authority. A PDF, Word file, scan or email on its own is not an e-invoice.

Is e-invoicing mandatory in the UAE?

Yes, for business-to-business (B2B) and business-to-government (B2G) transactions, in phases. Businesses with revenue of AED 50 million or more must issue e-invoices from 1 January 2027, businesses below AED 50 million from 1 July 2027, and government entities from 1 October 2027. Business-to-consumer (B2C) sales are excluded. See the full timeline.

When do I need to appoint an Accredited Service Provider?

Businesses with revenue below AED 50 million, and government entities, must appoint an ASP by 31 March 2027. The deadline for businesses with revenue of AED 50 million or more was 30 October 2026.

Does UAE e-invoicing apply to small businesses?

Yes. There is no small-business exemption for B2B and B2G transactions. Businesses with revenue below AED 50 million follow the second phase: appoint an ASP by 31 March 2027 and issue e-invoices from 1 July 2027.

We're not VAT registered. Does e-invoicing apply to us?

In most cases, yes. The Federal Tax Authority states that e-invoicing covers B2B and B2G transactions regardless of VAT registration. Non-VAT-registered businesses use a Tax Identification Number (TIN). A business that only sells to consumers still needs an ASP if it receives B2B invoices from its suppliers. Your readiness check will confirm where you stand.

How does the ASP connection work?

You create invoices in Finasheet, or in your connected system. Your invoice data goes to our ASP partner in the PINT-AE format; the ASP checks it, sends it over the Peppol network to your customer's ASP, and reports the tax data to the Federal Tax Authority. Supplier e-invoices sent to you arrive the same way. You use the same ASP for sending and receiving.

Is Finasheet an accredited service provider?

No. Finasheet is an accounting software and services company. E-invoices are exchanged and reported through our ASP partner, which is accredited by the UAE Ministry of Finance. We onboard you to our partner as part of your plan and share the partner's details during your readiness check.

What counts towards the 1,500 documents?

Every document exchanged in the PINT-AE format counts as one: sales invoices you send, purchase invoices you receive, and credit notes. A transmission that fails because of incorrect information you provided also counts.
