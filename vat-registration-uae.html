Questions

Frequently asked questions

When is VAT registration mandatory in the UAE?+
It becomes mandatory for resident businesses when taxable supplies and imports exceed AED 375,000. Voluntary registration is available once they exceed AED 187,500. We confirm your position before you apply.
How long does VAT registration take?+
Timelines depend on the FTA and how complete your documents are. We help you prepare everything correctly the first time to reduce avoidable delays.
What documents do I need?+
Typically your trade licence, owner ID documents, company documents, bank details and turnover figures. We give you a tailored checklist.
What happens after I get my TRN?+
You'll have a filing cycle to follow. We explain it clearly and can support your VAT setup and filing so the first return isn't a scramble.
Can you help if I registered incorrectly?+
Yes — we can review your existing registration and records and help you get the setup right going forward.
Do you guarantee approval?+
No one can guarantee an outcome. We provide FTA-aligned preparation based on the documents you provide, which gives your application the best footing.
