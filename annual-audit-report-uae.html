Where a licensed auditor is required, we support the accounting preparation and coordination process. We get your records and statements ready so the audit runs smoothly.
When should I start preparing?+
Earlier is always easier. Preparing records through the year — or at least well before the deadline — avoids the last-minute scramble.
My books are behind. Can you still help?+
Yes. We can run an accounting cleanup, reconcile your bank and close the year before preparing for reporting.
Do you prepare financial statements?+
We provide financial statement preparation support as part of getting your records audit-ready.
What documents will I need?+
Typically a full year of invoices, bills, bank statements, ledgers and previous statements. We give you a clear checklist and follow up on what's missing.
