Questions

Frequently asked questions

When are VAT returns due in the UAE?+
VAT returns are generally filed within 28 days from the end of the tax period. We aim to have your records ready well before that so filing is calm, not rushed.
My books are messy — can you still file?+
We'll review and, if needed, clean up your records first so the return is based on accurate figures rather than guesswork.
Do you check both input and output VAT?+
Yes. We review output VAT on sales and input VAT on purchases and expenses, and confirm the supporting documents.
Can you fix our VAT setup so this is easier each quarter?+
Yes — we can review your VAT categories, tax treatment and invoice format so the process becomes repeatable.
Do you guarantee zero errors or no penalties?+
We provide FTA-aligned preparation and review based on the documents you provide. We focus on careful, accurate preparation rather than making guarantees.
How is your fee decided?+
It depends on your VAT status, tax period and transaction volume, so we share a custom quote after a free review.
