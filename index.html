    FAQs

    Something else? Ask us on WhatsApp.

    What happens in a Books Status Check?

    We look at your actual records across five areas: monthly books, bank reconciliation, VAT, corporate tax and missing documents. You get a one-page summary that rates each one green, amber or red, with the next step. It's yours to keep, whether or not you work with us.

    How is the Clarity Score different?

    The score is a quick self-assessment from your own answers. The Books Status Check is done by us, on your real records.

    How do you price the work?

    On your monthly transactions, number of bank accounts, VAT and corporate tax status, and how far behind the books are. You get a written quote before any work starts.

    My books are months behind. Can you still help?

    Yes. We start with a catch-up to bring past months up to date, then move you onto monthly bookkeeping so it doesn't build up again.

    Do you work with free zone and mainland companies?

    Yes. What you need depends on your licence, business activity and registration status, which the check confirms.

    Do you carry out statutory audits?

    No. An audit must be signed by a licensed audit firm. We prepare your year-end books and schedules and coordinate with the auditor.

    Is our financial data safe with you?

    Our privacy policy explains how we use your records, who can access them and how long we keep them.
