What is UAE e-invoicing?

It is the UAE's system for issuing business invoices as structured data instead of PDFs or paper. Invoices are created in the PINT-AE format, exchanged through Accredited Service Providers (ASPs) over the Peppol network, and the tax data is reported to the Federal Tax Authority. A PDF, scan or email on its own is not an e-invoice.

Is e-invoicing mandatory in the UAE, and when?

Yes, for business-to-business (B2B) and business-to-government (B2G) transactions. Businesses with revenue of AED 50 million or more go live on 1 January 2027, businesses below AED 50 million on 1 July 2027 (ASP appointed by 31 March 2027), and government entities on 1 October 2027. Business-to-consumer (B2C) sales are excluded.

Does it apply to small businesses and non-VAT-registered businesses?

Yes. There is no small-business exemption for B2B and B2G transactions, and the Federal Tax Authority states that e-invoicing applies regardless of VAT registration. Non-VAT-registered businesses use a Tax Identification Number (TIN). A business that only sells to consumers still needs an ASP if it receives B2B invoices from suppliers.

Can we keep our current accounting software?

Often, yes. Finasheet can connect QuickBooks, Odoo, SAP, Oracle, Excel-based workflows and custom ERPs to our ASP partner. Integration is quoted separately because it depends on your system. Or move to Finasheet, where the accounting software is included in the annual plan.

Is Finasheet an accredited service provider?

No. Finasheet is an accounting software and services company. Your e-invoices are exchanged and reported through our ASP partner, which is accredited by the UAE Ministry of Finance. We onboard you to the partner as part of your plan and share the partner's details on your readiness call.

What counts towards the 1,500 e-invoices?

Each document exchanged in the PINT-AE format counts as one: sales invoices you send, purchase invoices you receive and credit notes. A transmission that fails because of incorrect information you provided also counts.

What happens if we use all 1,500?

You can top up from your e-invoicing dashboard at any time without changing plans. We walk you through the top-up options on your readiness call.

How much does Finasheet e-invoicing cost?

The annual plan is AED 1,500 per year. It includes Finasheet accounting software, e-invoicing, 1,500 documents a year, ASP onboarding assistance, a separate e-invoicing dashboard, portal training and technical support. Monthly billing is available at AED 150 per month. Connecting an existing ERP is quoted separately.

How long does it take to go live?

Standard setup on Finasheet typically takes one working day once we have your details. Connecting an existing system typically takes three to four working days. Finasheet customers can start exchanging e-invoices from 1 January 2027.

What are the penalties for not complying?

Under Cabinet Decision No. 173 of 2026, not implementing e-invoicing or not appointing an ASP on time can cost AED 5,000, then AED 5,000 a month, capped at AED 50,000 in any 12 months. Each e-invoice or credit note not issued on time can cost AED 100, capped at AED 5,000 a month.
