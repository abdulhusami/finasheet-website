Questions

Frequently asked questions

Does my UAE business need to register for corporate tax?+
Most UAE businesses are within scope of corporate tax. We review your trade licence, activity and structure and confirm your registration requirement.
What is the corporate tax rate in the UAE?+
It is 0% on taxable income up to AED 375,000 and 9% on taxable income above that. We help you register correctly so filing later is straightforward.
What documents do I need to register?+
Typically your trade licence, owner ID documents, ownership documents, authorized signatory details and business activity details. We provide a tailored checklist.
I registered myself — should I still get it reviewed?+
Yes, a quick review helps confirm your details match your licence and that your records will be ready when filing is due.
Do you also handle the filing afterwards?+
Yes. We can prepare your records and support corporate tax filing when the time comes.
Are there matters you don't handle directly?+
Specialized tax-agent or legal matters may require appropriately licensed professional involvement. We focus on accounting preparation, registration support and coordination.
